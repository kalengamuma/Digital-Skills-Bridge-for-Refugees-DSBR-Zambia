Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

What drives DSBR and the principles we never compromise on.

Our Vision

“A world where every refugee has equal access to digital skills, economic opportunity, and the freedom to build a self-reliant future.”

Our Mission

“To equip refugees and displaced persons in Zambia with practical, certified digital skills that open doors to employment, freelancing, and entrepreneurship — through accessible, community-centred learning.”

Core Values

🔓 Accessibility

No learner excluded because of connectivity, device type, or literacy level. We design for the least equipped.

🛠 Practicality

Every skill taught has a direct and immediate application to income, employment, or self-employment.

📜 Certification

Every graduate receives a verifiable certificate. Skills recognition is central — it turns learning into a credential.

Contribution to Global Goals

SDG 4

Quality Education — structured, accessible, certified learning for displaced persons.

SDG 8

Decent Work & Economic Growth — digital skills linked to employment and income.

SDG 10

Reduced Inequalities — closing the digital and economic divide for the most vulnerable.

SDG 17

Partnerships for the Goals — multi-stakeholder model connecting NGOs, tech and communities.

Also aligned with the Global Compact on Refugees (2018): education, livelihoods and self-reliance pillars.
