Empowering Refugees Through Digital Education & Economic Independence

The Digital Skills Bridge for Refugees (DSBR) delivers practical, certified ICT education to refugees and displaced persons across Meheba, Mantapala, Mayukwayukwa and Lusaka — through mobile app, SMS, web platform and community training.

Explore Programs Volunteer Support Us

Why Digital Skills Matter

In a world where employment and opportunity live online, digital exclusion is itself a form of poverty. DSBR breaks the cycle of dependency.

📱

Mobile App

Full courses, progress tracking, quizzes and offline downloads for low-connectivity environments.

💬

SMS Micro-Lessons

Daily bite-sized lessons via SMS — no internet or smartphone required. Maximum reach.

🌐

Web Platform

Structured courses, educator dashboards, learner management and partner analytics.

🏫

Community Training

In-person facilitated sessions at settlement centres with hands-on equipment and peer learning.
