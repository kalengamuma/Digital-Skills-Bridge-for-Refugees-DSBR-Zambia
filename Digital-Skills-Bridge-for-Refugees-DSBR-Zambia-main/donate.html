Donate to DSBR

Your contribution directly funds platform development, community training equipment, facilitator stipends, curriculum materials and learner support across Zambia’s refugee settlements.

What Your Support Enables

  • Android app & web platform hosting
  • Laptops, tablets & projectors for centres
  • Community facilitator training & stipends
  • Printed workbooks & SMS delivery costs
  • Monitoring, evaluation & impact reporting

Suggested Impact Levels

  • $50 — Materials for 5 learners
  • $200 — One week of community training
  • $500 — Full module for 10 learners
  • $2,000 — Pilot cohort support
