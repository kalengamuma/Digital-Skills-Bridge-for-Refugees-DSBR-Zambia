Learning Pathways

Our Programs

From digital literacy to freelancing and entrepreneurship — a clear pathway from exclusion to economic independence.

📘

Digital Literacy

Smartphone and computer fundamentals, internet browsing, online safety, email, WhatsApp and Google tools. Built for absolute beginners.

💻

Computer Fundamentals

Hardware, operating systems, file management, typing skills and confident navigation of Windows and Android devices.

📊

Microsoft Office

Word, Excel and PowerPoint — professional documents, spreadsheets, formulas, charts and presentations for office-ready skills.

🌐

Internet & Email

Effective searching, professional Gmail use, attachments, Google Drive collaboration and digital communication etiquette.

🐍

Python Programming

Introduction to coding logic, basic Python syntax and practical scripts — a foundation for further technical learning.

🌍

Web Development

HTML, CSS and building simple webpages. Learners create a personal portfolio site they can show to employers and clients.

📄

Career Readiness

CV writing, LinkedIn profiles, job search strategies, cover letters and professional communication for employment and freelancing.

🚀

Entrepreneurship

Business planning, digital marketing, financial literacy, mobile money for business and launching micro-enterprises in settlement contexts.

How Learning Is Delivered

📱 Mobile App

Full course access, progress tracking, quizzes, certificates and offline content for low-connectivity environments.

💬 SMS Lessons

Daily bite-sized lessons via SMS — no internet or smartphone required. Maximum reach into remote learners.

🌐 Web Platform

Structured courses, educator dashboards, learner management and partner analytics.

🏫 Community Sessions

In-person facilitated training at settlement centres with hands-on equipment and peer learning.
