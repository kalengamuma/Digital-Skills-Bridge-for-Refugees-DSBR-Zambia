About DSBR

Bridging the Digital Divide for Refugees in Zambia

A structured, multi-channel digital learning platform built specifically for refugees and displaced persons.

Our Story

Zambia hosts one of sub-Saharan Africa’s largest and most established refugee populations — over 110,000 registered refugees and asylum seekers, primarily from the Democratic Republic of Congo, Rwanda, Burundi and Somalia. They are settled across Meheba, Mantapala, Mayukwayukwa and urban areas of Lusaka.

Despite legal rights under the Refugee Act of 2017, the gap between rights and lived reality remains wide. Infrastructure, education and economic opportunity in settlements are severely limited. At the same time, the global shift toward digital work has created an unprecedented opportunity — platforms like Upwork and Fiverr connect workers worldwide with employers, and many in-demand tasks require only weeks of structured training.

DSBR was created to bridge that gap — between the opportunity of the digital economy and the reality facing most refugees: no structured digital education, limited device skills, and no clear pathway into online income.

Why We Started

Refugees face a convergence of barriers that systematically exclude them from digital and economic opportunity.

Digital Exclusion

Most refugees in settlements have never received structured ICT training. Access to computers, electricity and reliable internet is extremely limited.

Employment Barriers

Formal employment often requires work permits many struggle to obtain. Without certified digital skills, refugees remain invisible to online employers.

Structural & Gender Barriers

Women and girls face compounded barriers. Youth — the largest demographic — have the fewest structured educational and vocational pathways.

Cycle of Dependency

Without skills there is no income; without income, dependence on humanitarian assistance continues. DSBR interrupts this cycle at its root.

Our Values

🔓 Accessibility

No learner excluded because of connectivity, device type or literacy level. We design for the least equipped.

🛠 Practicality

Every skill taught has a direct and immediate application to income, employment or self-employment.

📜 Certification

Every graduate receives a verifiable certificate. Skills recognition turns learning into a credential.
