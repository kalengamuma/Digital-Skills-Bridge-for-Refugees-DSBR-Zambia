Partnership Opportunities

DSBR is an open submission. We welcome engagement from any organisation that shares our commitment to refugee dignity, digital inclusion and economic empowerment.

Ways to Partner

Funding Partners

Grant funding and project financing for platform development, equipment, staffing and operations. Full reporting and co-branding available.

Institutional Partners

Endorsement, community entry facilitation, beneficiary referrals, settlement access and co-implementation support.

Technical & Certification

Curriculum review, formal recognition of certificates, software access, mentorship and industry-relevant content.

Employment Linkages

Job placement, internship pipelines, freelancing platform partnerships and remote employer connections for graduates.

Prospective Partners

We seek alignment with organisations already working in refugee education, livelihoods and digital inclusion.

Why Partner with DSBR

Designed for the last mile

Built specifically for refugees in low-connectivity, low-resource environments — not adapted from urban programmes.

Multi-channel delivery

SMS, mobile app, web and in-person — impact is not limited by infrastructure gaps.

Certified outcomes

Every graduate receives a verifiable certificate, giving partners measurable, credible impact to report.

Gender & youth focus

60%+ female enrolment target built in — not an afterthought.
